## Nonblocking limitations

1. Exported `voice-bindings.json` includes each clip's bound `bytes` receipt field, while the raw input `VoiceBindingSchema` is strict and rejects that extra field. The bundle is inspectable/playable/portable, but not directly re-plannable without removing `bytes` and resolving its relative audio paths from the bundle directory. Direct round-trip import was not a mandatory condition, and documentation does not claim it. Prefer a dedicated input-compatible bindings export in future work; do not advertise effortless round-trip editing now.
2. A process killed during export may leave the exclusively reserved outer directory and staging/owner files. Only the complete `bundle` is published atomically; callers must use a new destination after a crash. This does not expose a successful partial bundle or violate the no-overwrite contract. Same-destination crash cleanup/resume was not promised.
3. Current story recordings are absent. The asset closure demonstrates sound capture/provenance/measurement with a synthetic tone and leaves actual narration pending. It does not complete new voice production, actual speech verification, full animation/video rendering, perceptual synchronization or final creative acceptance.

These limitations do not require a new implementation candidate for this contract. Any later code or frozen artifact changes invalidate this exact-candidate verdict and require the affected checks again.
