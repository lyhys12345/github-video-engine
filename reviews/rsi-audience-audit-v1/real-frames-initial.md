Reviewed only the contract, the two phone contact sheets (ArtCraft global frames 127, 136, 150, 158, 170, 178, 197; OpenMAIC 270, 300, 313, 350, 390), ArtCraft source action contact sheet (source frames 20, 49, 60, 77, 78, 98), and OpenMAIC source stills 261, 304, 350. No machine audience-audit or other judge report was read before these observations. These are sampled stills, not continuous playback or audio review. Caption ranges below are display clocks supplied with the task, not verified speech timing. This assesses audience-audit usefulness, not overall video approval.

## Phone presentation and embedded UI

- Across these sampled frames the large Chinese bottom captions are readily distinguishable at the supplied phone-frame scale, with no apparent clipping or overlap. The ArtCraft top action labels are also readable where bright; the frame 158 label appears dimmer. Tiny source/disclaimer text and page counters are substantially harder to read on a phone. No conclusion is made about all unsampled frames or measured actual device viewing conditions.

- OpenMAIC's upper full-screen source interface is too small to read its question or right-side conversation reliably at phone scale. The enlarged lower discussion excerpt makes MAYA's message and Stop Discussion much more legible, although its heading is intentionally truncated with an ellipsis and the smallest labels remain small. The excerpt does not enlarge the left question itself. No clear caption overlap or crop in these stills.

## Limits

Visual judgments refer to the supplied images and sampled instants. Source stills establish what source UI contains at larger resolution, not legibility in the phone composite. No audio was heard, no continuous video was inspected, and neither narration timing nor unsampled transitions are verified. There is no claim about the full video or an automated audit's correctness until its output is separately compared.
