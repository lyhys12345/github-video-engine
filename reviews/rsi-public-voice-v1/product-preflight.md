# Public voice product preflight

Preliminary observations only; this is not a candidate verdict. Inspected the RSI contract, public-local-voice-v1 onboarding draft, first-owner quickstart, and github-explainer skill.

The onboarding draft distinguishes public presets from private owner voices, exact approval, and offline/export limits. A first-time reader of the standalone setup needs the engine's Node 24 and pinned pnpm installation, browser/FFmpeg requirements, and a clear check that the Python launcher actually selects the documented 3.12.14. The root reported that the onboarding draft was updated to address those points; this preflight did not independently verify that update.

For final review, supply a frozen candidate manifest and exact evidence paths, including isolated install and public model inventory/provenance; CLI receipts for absent, stale, and valid test-only approvals; measured original speech; FFprobe and full decode; public-only export, offline import/reuse and changed-scene behavior; and representative rendered frame files with timestamps.
