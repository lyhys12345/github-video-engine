# Narrated narrative technical excerpts

`explanation narrative-audio-preview --task <current narrative task> --spec <audio mapping> --data <state> --out <new directory>` assembles a short narrated technical excerpt. `narrative-audio-status` and `narrative-audio-export` use the resulting task ID. This separate contract preserves historical silent/reset/source-clock tasks.

The mapping contains an exact source-clock visual selection plus one complete local TTS-cache recording per selected supported scene. Every transcript must exactly equal the entire current scene narration, which the narrative contract in turn binds to the complete linked Story shots. Changing prose therefore requires a legal new Story/narrative/preview mapping; an old receipt cannot be relabelled. The optional historicalNarrativeTaskId retains the earlier complete plan and receipt as history from the same pinned source, never current review.

Audio is decoded with an explicit48kHz sample budget, independent of the older24fps presentation clock. Narrative output is30fps,1600samples/frame. Complete non-silent original-speed clips must fit their output scene interval including authored offset. No automatic truncation, stretching, cross-scene spill or source-clock audio slicing is performed. Lead and unresolved NOT PRODUCIBLE cards contain digital silence and no voice-caption cues. These gaps remain unproduced even though their visible status cards are encoded.

Caption character ranges must partition the entire exact transcript. Recording-relative sample timing must stay within the complete clip and scene, with representable nonoverlapping projected frames and readability bounds. The exported VTT and ledger use48kHz sample positions; subtitle display is selectable external VTT/HTML track and an MP4 mov_text track. Captions are not burned into or allowed to cover the source frame by this renderer. The player decides selectable subtitle positioning. Speech content, alignment, transcript accuracy, voice identity, natural listening and continuous perceptual quality remain unverified.

The new pending fact-review packet binds current narrative text, full visual/source closure, physical timeline, complete audio receipts/generation references, decoded PCM placements, captions, environment and actual movie identity. Exports retain raw recordings, receipts, generation manifests where present, mixed PCM and caption/frame ledgers. Edits produce different task/packet identities and remain pending. The deterministic checks provide integrity and timing evidence, not semantic truth or approval. Source interpretation, shortened demonstration scripts and revision choices require human/session-agent authorship. No paid generation, target repository execution or finished formal full film is part of this slice.
