## Content and timing contract

The Agent authors meaningful chapters, exact sourced claims, narration, visuals/choreography, scene timing and cue partitions. The engine never writes more content, repeats clips, inserts duration padding, stretches speech or trims a recording to reach a target length. Every narrative scene must remain in original order; every authored source shot must be included whole and exactly once. Every produced scene requires exactly one full current recording/caption partition; evidence/unsupported scenes remain visible, silent and unproduced. Total authored frame ranges plus lead/gaps must fit300seconds or the operation refuses to render.

Each supported scene's exact `storyTaskId`/`expectedGraphHash` fields use `$SCENE_STORY_TASK_ID` and `$SCENE_STORY_GRAPH_HASH`. The runner derives an explicitly distinct source Story and matching animation subset for that scene and binds their actual committed hashes in the complete parent plan. A documented closing may itself be a source-backed Story scene, so a four-second summary card is not mandatory. In complete mode document-card.json is not required. Generic diagrams are supported; existing short audience/card workflows keep their own unchanged constraints.

The source capture remains immutable, including its initial authoring defaults. Selecting version2 explicitly selects this separate at-most300second experimental narrative budget rather than rewriting the earlier source request. The author snapshot, selected profile/code hash, current plan, exact whole-shot frame ranges, physical mixed-PCM placements and actual movie are bound and inspectable. The upper bound is a resource/scope limit, not a request to create a five-minute film.

Huihui remains the existing local Windows prototype: one Chinese paragraph per scene, at most120Unicode points/30seconds per generation. Longer prose must be explicitly divided into additional authored scenes. Captions keep the existing character/readability/sample bounds and three-line reserved display area; a long transcript needs explicit cue partitions, not truncation. Complete recordings must fit authored scenes. `--through voice` exposes complete-mode generation task IDs, import fingerprints and measured sample counts to support explicit author revisions. Listening and word alignment remain unverified.

## Scene-level cache behavior

Story/asset/source-preview tasks are derived per scene. A change to one scene's choreography regenerates that source movie and the final assembly while retaining other scene movies and unchanged recordings. Shared evidence, timing configuration or renderer-code changes can legitimately invalidate more tasks. A narration edit requires its own new complete recording; the engine does not reuse a mismatched transcript. Every candidate/attempt preserves the previous authored inputs, results and failures.

## Remaining limits

This is a complete-coverage technical preview, not audience-finished production. The generic source renderer still uses diagnostic diagrams/banners; parent titles/screen-text/transitions remain editable/exported where the renderer does not implement them. It does not claim long-range state continuity between reset scene boundaries. Semantic source interpretation, transcript accuracy, natural listening, word alignment, continuous perceptual AV and final owner acceptance remain pending. No Judge, full regression, paid API, source-repository execution, deployment or formal full film was run, and these checks do not establish90% product readiness.
