Captions use text-weighted allocation within the measured continuous recording. The JSON records source-time and output-frame windows, and the SRT and burned-in captions share that cue table. Filenames, spoken decimal words and common mechanism terms remain together; small final lines are balanced. Timing is explicitly estimated, not forced alignment. Listening, exact spoken synchronization, pronunciation, voice naturalness, continuous audiovisual rhythm and final human acceptance remain pending. Historical G4 UNVERIFIED records are preserved.

New renders may instead provide `cueTimingPath` in the segment input. The strict timing sidecar uses `schemaVersion: "1.1"` and binds the exact audio SHA-256, transcript SHA-256, measured duration and ordered `captionTextSha256` array. Each array entry is the SHA-256 of that phase's raw `captionText` UTF-8 bytes, without Unicode, whitespace or punctuation normalization. One boundary per caption phase plus the final audio boundary is required. The declared methods are `manual-listening` and `forced-alignment-reviewed`. Stale, missing, reordered or mismatched hashes, changed segmentation even with identical concatenated text, duration drift, missing/non-monotonic boundaries and sub-four-frame cues fail before rendering. Both the renderer and direct timeline entry validate the sidecar.

These methods record a caller declaration, not an independent timing verdict. The timeline labels them `caller-declared-manual-listening-boundaries` or `caller-declared-forced-alignment-reviewed-boundaries`; cue confidence is `caller-declared-reviewed-timing-independent-unverified`. Its `timingEvidence` preserves the caller's `reviewBasis`, identifies the source as `caller-declared`, and records `independentTimingReview: "UNVERIFIED"` and `ownerAcceptance: "pending"`. Render evaluation reports `caller-declared-boundaries-independent-unverified-owner-pending`. The sidecar and its hash are copied into the output and fingerprint. A timing-only edit invalidates the timeline, captions, frames, MP4 and review approval. No transcription accuracy, voice quality, independent synchronization approval or final owner acceptance follows from a sidecar. Omitting it preserves the explicitly estimated text-weighted fallback.

Legacy timing schema 1.0 is rejected without automatic promotion or estimated fallback. Migration requires reviewing the boundaries again against the exact current caption phases and recording that basis in a new 1.1 sidecar. Automatically adding phase hashes or changing the version does not establish that review. Historical outputs and UNVERIFIED reports remain unchanged.
