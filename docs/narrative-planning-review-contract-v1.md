# Narrative planning engineering review contract

Frozen-slice scope: URL-bound complete authored story planning JSON, existing Story-planner links, complete written fact-review coverage and editable export. No new media/whole-video assembly/frame extraction. Independent active roles: product, reliability, evidence, content_facts and security; video_quality is active only for authored storyboard scope and must not claim new media/AV observations. All roles review the same exact frozen candidate; missing observations are UNVERIFIED and failures are preserved.

- P1: Two materially different real GitHub sources produce complete five-role authored plans and at least one exact source-bound supported Story child each; all unresolved scenes remain in the exported full story.
- F1: Every thesis, narration, visible text, transition, limitation, closing and execution disposition is included in explicit required review coverage. Real independent receipts bind exact plan and packet hashes, retain identity-not-authenticated/approval-none, and cannot resolve needs-evidence or needs-adapter scene states.
- R1: Story links verify full source binding, graph hash, selected shots and complete narration/visual/claim/citation identity. Inputs/receipts and state can move; offline status/export reverify committed closure. Source CAS corruption and stale review edits fail closed.
- E1: Source directory/ZIP/manifest match raw bytes; actual GitHub captures and agent authorship are distinguished from mocks, structural tests and historical renderer support. Exact inputs/outputs, real independent reports and test results are preserved without invented full-video or completion claims.
- S1: Sources remain inert data; no target code, paid API or publication. Source files are taken from the verified capture, not caller-controlled paths or linked arbitrary roots. Narrative scene IDs and export paths are bounded; no review attribution becomes trust/authority.
- V1: Review authored storyboard structure and explicit unresolved capabilities only. Estimated durations do not establish measured voice timing, continuity or retention. No new frame/audio/video observation is claimed; actual AV and owner/G6 acceptance remain pending.
